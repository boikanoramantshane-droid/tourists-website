About Lesotho

Lesotho is a small but breathtaking mountain kindom surrounded entirely by South Africa. It is known as the kingdom in the sky because it is the only country in the world that lies entirely above 1000 metres above the sea level. For tourists looking for adventure, beautiful scenery, rich culture and unforgettable experiences, Lesotho is a destination worth exploring!

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote Lesotho as a beautiful and unforgettable tourist destination by showcasing its amazing landscapes, rich Basotho culture , historical attractions and exiting activities. We aim to provide tourist with useful information, inspire them to explore the Kindom in the Sky, and encourage responsible tourism while supporting local communities.
