Did You Know?

What can tourists do in Lesotho?
Visitors can enjoy horse riding, hiking, skiing, sightseeing, fishing and exploring beautiful waterfalls.
What makes Lesotho special?
Lesotho is unique because it is completely surrounded by South Africa. It is also famous for its mountains, traditional Basotho blankets, ponies and rich cultural heritage.
Why is Lesotho called the Kingdom in the Sky?
Lesotho is known as the Kingdom in the Sky because of its high altitude and mountainous landscape. It is one of the highest countries in the world.
